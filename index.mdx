## Make your first REST API request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your API credentials" icon="shield-keyhole">
    Follow [Authentication](/credentials) to generate an API Key in the Bridge Admin Console. Ask your Bridge administrator for access if needed. Keep the key on your server.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the connection" icon="code">
    Call the authenticated [health check](/api-reference/health/health-check) at `https://api-connect-us.bridge.new/bridge/api/health` with your `x-api-key` header.

    Then choose the endpoint for your task in the **REST API** tab. Each endpoint includes its request fields, responses, and examples.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Lead Capture uses a separate endpoint and a public `captureKey`. Follow its [integration guide](/lead-capture/integration); a `202` means the lead was accepted and queued, not that a WhatsApp message was sent.
</Note>

## Build from the published contract

- [Onboarding template parameters](/templates-and-parameters) — open conversations with WhatsApp templates and supply their variables.
