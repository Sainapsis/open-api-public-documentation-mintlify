## Supported template categories

The template that opens the conversation can be either a **utility** or a **marketing** template:

- **Utility templates**: Strictly transactional, echoing what the visitor just submitted (such as confirming receipt of an enquiry). These qualify for lower WhatsApp conversation rates.
- **Marketing templates**: May include promotional language, discounts, special offers, or direct sales pitches. These are billed at standard WhatsApp marketing conversation rates.

<Note>
  **Authentication** templates cannot be used for lead capture. Any template configured with the `AUTHENTICATION` category will be rejected.
</Note>

You configure and submit your templates directly in Meta WhatsApp Manager, and getting them approved is a prerequisite for going live.

## Meta classification and deliverability

<Warning>
  Meta classifies templates by their content, not strictly by the category you select during submission. If a template submitted as UTILITY contains promotional language, Meta may reclassify it as MARKETING or reject it.
</Warning>

Choosing the right category upfront prevents unintended classification issues and ensures expected deliverability and billing.

## Category examples

**Utility (Transactional)** — acknowledges an enquiry without promotional offers:

```text
Hi {{1}}, thanks for your enquiry about the {{2}}. A member of our team will follow up here shortly.
```

**Marketing (Promotional)** — includes promotional language, discounts, or sales offers:

```text
Hi {{1}}, thanks for your enquiry about the {{2}}. Book this week and get 15% off — reply YES to claim.
```

## Practical guidance

- **Choose Utility** when your primary goal is rapid transactional follow-up confirming the visitor's request. Keep it strictly focused on echoing their submission without promotional hooks to maintain the utility category and lower messaging rate.
- **Choose Marketing** when you want to immediately provide introductory discounts, promotional incentives, or catalog offers upon capture.
- **Avoid Authentication templates**: Authentication templates are intended exclusively for one-time passwords and verification codes, and are rejected for campaign lead dispatches.
- Once the person replies, you are in a normal conversation session and template category constraints no longer apply to incoming or agent responses.
